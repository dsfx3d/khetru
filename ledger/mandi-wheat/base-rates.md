# Mandi wheat base rates from IMD observed rainfall

Generated by `evidence observe base-rates`; do not edit. Every figure comes from the
committed files named below, with the development rule values. The owner chooses the
bundle v1 values at U8. No forecast enters this table.

## Inputs

- Observations: IMD 0.25° gridded daily rainfall, vintage `final-r20261010`,
  35 files under `observations/`,
  set digest `586a83894a58c369cae9587b93f90f5a67b53365b1647baefb83d55d8cf63105`.
- Band map: `bands/band-map.csv`, SHA-256
  `3b7d40b946de3cb7053954a77801f3d22e2d1935470ef66f930ae8b52a02763d`.
- Seasons: 1991 to 2025 (35).
- Issue dates: each Monday from 15 Oct to 15 Nov;
  the observed window is the 7 IMD dates starting 2 days after it
  (IMD's date D is the 24 hours ending 08:30 IST on D).
- Coverage share 1; wet day 1 mm; dry-day gap 3 days;
  climatology ±7 days with pseudo-count 0.5; sowing cutoff 30 Nov.
