# Mandi wheat power simulation: recount for issue dates to 7 Dec

Generated by `evidence bundle power --variant december-7`; do not edit. The procedure is
`docs/findings/2026-10-mandi-power-simulation-procedure.md`. Outcomes and climatology come
from the committed IMD files named below, with the development rule values except as noted below; forecasts are
simulated at a stated true skill. No real forecast enters this table.

This is a recount made on 2026-10-11, after the first run, whose table is `power.md`, and
after the recount `power-december.md`. Both tables are unchanged. It is
listed under "Changes after the run" in the procedure. Two development
values are changed in code: the last issue date is 7 Dec
where the first run has 15 Nov, and the sowing cutoff is 16 Dec where it has
30 Nov. Every other value is the first run's, read from `bundles/dev`, which holds
neither changed value. The development bundle `bundles/dev-2` holds the same two, for
issuing claims; this table does not read it. The exploratory views named in the
variant are counted after the verdict band.

Each simulation (one area, event, threshold, season range, true skill and kind of errors)
has a seed of its own, and so has each cell of "live seasons until a band can be judged".
No seed is shared with an earlier table or between the two areas. The confidence and
margin rows of one simulation read the same simulated records.

The issue dates are the owner's choice, made on 2026-10-11 after reading `power-december.md`:
each Monday from 15 Oct to 7 Dec. They are a starting value for bundle v1 and not a
frozen one: the owner freezes the bundle's values at the tag. The cutoff of 16 Dec is still
the agent's choice and open for the owner.

  use (10 Oct to 22 Dec at the widest).
