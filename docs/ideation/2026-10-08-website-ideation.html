Grounding Context

Codebase Context
  • The repo has no code yet. It holds only STRATEGY.md and .scratch/ notes: the handoff doc and two council transcripts.
  • Role of the website. STRATEGY.md:56: "Website proof of concept, then an Android app as the farmer MVP; Hindi/Pahari voice and icons; low bandwidth; FPO and KVK channels." The handoff doc (khetru.md) still calls Stage 1 a full "responsive website" with farm mapping. The two docs conflict.
  • Who will actually visit. The councils say web visitors will be agronomists, NGOs, developers and FPO staff, and that "low-literacy feature-phone farmers won't use a website first" (council-q1:25). Phones are "shared and read aloud by a son" (council-q4:45).
  • What the website can prove. The website has "no stable identity", so metrics that need identity start with Android. "The PoC website sets baselines for metric 3 only" (council-q4:82). That is claim reliability, scored per altitude band. "plot-level calibration is fiction in the hills" (council-q4:70).
  • Boundaries. No officer dashboards. KVK and FPO are channels only. No in-app decision prompts before 5000 farmers return. No claim the Evidence track can't verify. Don't make farmers work for khetru. No stored farm coordinates without consent.
  • The crux. "If honest data can't beat the dealer on one decision, six more sources won't help" (council-q1:31). Also: "'Honest uncertainty' might mean 'we don't know' most of the time, so why return?" (council-q1:25).
External context (web research, moderate value)
  • Government apps. Kisan Suvidha, Meghdoot and mKisan are text-heavy and have no voice. They give regional weather but no decision for a specific field.
  • Soil Health Card (SHC). Farmers don't adopt it because they don't understand or trust the card and it has no timing guidance. About 40% follow the dose (IDinsight; Gujarat AERC).
  • IMD agromet advisories (GKMS). Block-level advisories go out Tue/Fri through KVK agromet units. In Himachal, the Nauni and Palampur agromet units reach about 5,000 farmers through 50 WhatsApp groups that Krishi Sakhis spread.
  • Prior art. Digital Green's Farmer.Chat started with extension workers first and used voice. Kisan e-Mitra uses Bhashini for voice.
  • Uncertainty research. Deterministic advice helps only when the forecast beats climatology. Framing like "in X of the last 10 years" draws on farmers' own memory.

I1. Public claim ledger with a pass/fail season

I1Axis 5 · What the PoC provesConfidence 80%Complexity Medium
Description
Each call the website makes is published before its window as a dated claim that can be proven wrong. Each claim records band, crop stage, verdict, confidence and expiry. Every week the claims are scored against IMD and satellite observations, and an honest "can't tell" counts as a scored outcome. Each claim is scored against two baselines the farmer already has for free: climatology and the IMD/GKMS block advisory. The site states its pass/fail rule in public before the season starts, for example: "if our band-level sow/spray calls don't beat the crop calendar by end of rabi 2027, we say so here." A backtest on past seasons, clearly labelled as hindcast, means the ledger doesn't open at zero. Each call card links to its ledger entry and shows the band's record for that decision type.
Basis
  • direct: "Claim reliability - share of khetru's falsifiable claims that held, scored so stated uncertainty and honest 'can't tell' count; weekly, server-side against public observations per altitude band, no user data." (STRATEGY.md:32)
  • direct: "it is where 'backed by results' comes from, and it gates the chat assistant and any new data domains." (STRATEGY.md:52)
  • direct: "The PoC website sets baselines for metric 3 only." (council-q4:82)
  • external: GKMS block advisories are the incumbent free baseline. "Skill over climatology" is how weather services verify forecasts (NWS). Clinical AI runs a "shadow mode" before go-live.
Rationale
Five generators, working independently, converged on this idea. It is the one thing the website can honestly prove given who visits it. The output is the asset everything else waits on: the chat assistant, new data domains, the "backed by results" claim, and a go/no-go for the Android app. Agronomist and NGO visitors are exactly the gatekeepers who need a visible record before they pass khetru on.
Downsides
It needs a scoring rule for GKMS's free-text advisories. Early in the season the record is thin, so the site needs an honest "too early to tell" state. A public miss rate can hurt adoption before it builds trust. The backtest must never be presented as a live record.

I2. Climatology-first call with a fixed verdict set

I2Axis 3 · Trust surfaceConfidence 75%Complexity Medium
Description
By default the page shows what the farmer can check against their own memory: "In 7 of the last 10 years, rain came within 3 days of this date in your band." khetru gives a sharper call only for bands and decisions where its forecast has been shown to beat that baseline. Every call ends on one of a small fixed set of verdicts: Do it · Wait · Same as your Soil Health Card · Can't tell. "Can't tell" always comes with a reason, such as "station too far" or "card too old". Every number carries a source chip: lab-measured (dated), satellite estimate, or forecast.
Basis
  • direct: "'Honest uncertainty' might mean 'we don't know' most of the time, so why return?" (council-q1:25)
  • direct: "Risk: khetru may just repeat the SHC." (council-q4:27). "'Wait, don't spray yet' is a decision, so inaction must count." (council-q4:67)
  • direct: "Satellite estimates must be clearly distinguished from direct field measurements." (khetru.md:211)
  • external: Smallholder-forecast research says deterministic advice helps only when the forecast beats climatology, and that framing anchored in farmers' memory works. Prescrire, the ad-free drug bulletin that competes with pharma reps, routinely publishes "nothing new" verdicts.
Rationale
This turns the two worries the councils raised into features. Saying "can't tell" becomes useful, because the farmer still gets a baseline they can check themselves. Saying "same as your card" becomes a trust-building verdict, not a failure. A fixed verdict set also gives the I1 ledger a finite set of outcomes to score. No doc specifies the output format yet, so this is the decision to make.
Downsides
It needs 10+ years of station or reanalysis history per band, and Himachal data is sparse. In weeks where khetru shows no skill, the site will say "the calendar is as good as we are", which is honest but hard to sell. Wording the verdicts in Hindi and Pahari needs the planned farmer test.

I3. Band-level call permalinks: precomputed, static, no coordinates

I3Axis 2 · The one-field decision callConfidence 70%Complexity Medium
Description
Drop GPS pins and boundary drawing from the website. The farmer (or whoever holds the phone) picks a village, which resolves to an altitude band, then a crop. The page shows only the one money-at-risk decision currently live for that band, or says that no decision is live. A scheduled job precomputes every band × crop × stage × week cell as a static page, e.g. /kangra/900-1200/wheat/sow/2026-w42. Each URL is a frozen, timestamped claim with an expiry. The same URL is the page, the forwardable card (I4) and the ledger entry (I1). There is no login, no session and no per-visitor compute.
Basis
  • direct: "Calibrate per altitude band and reward an honest 'can't tell', because plot-level calibration is fiction in the hills." (council-q4:70)
  • direct: "In sparse hill districts, crop + timing + district can re-identify a farmer." (council-q4:73). "no stored farm coordinates or cross-season linking without consent." (STRATEGY.md:38)
  • direct: "Metrics 1 and 4 start only with the Android app because the website has no stable identity." (council-q4:30). "Drops if khetru becomes browsing." (council-q4:36)
  • external: Mountain avalanche bulletins give coarse regional data per elevation band, not per slope, and ask nothing of the reader.
Rationale
The unit the call is made for, the unit the Evidence track scores, and the unit privacy protects all become one cell. Privacy then holds by construction, not by policy. It is cheap and fast on 2G, fits a tiny OSS team, and every page can be forwarded. It also settles the conflict between the farm-mapping Stage 1 in khetru.md and the PoC framing in STRATEGY.
Downsides
Answers are coarser than a plot-level view, a trade the councils accept. A band × crop × stage grid can grow large. Aspect (sunny or shady slope) was dropped because the Evidence track can't calibrate it at band level. The village-to-band mapping must handle villages that span bands.

I4. Voice-and-icon call card for Sakhi WhatsApp groups

I4Axis 4 · Language, literacy & bandwidthConfidence 55%Complexity Medium
Description
Treat the website's main farmer-facing output as a card, not a visit. Each call (I3) renders as an image with a large verdict icon plus a 20-second Hindi/Pahari voice note. The script is written to be read or played aloud to someone else, for example by a son to an elder or by a Krishi Sakhi to a group. The card is sized for the WhatsApp groups Himachal's agromet units already reach through Sakhis. Scope is one district and one crop. The intermediary only forwards the card: no dashboard, no login, no farmer data.
Basis
  • direct: "low-literacy feature-phone farmers won't use a website first. The web-first path fits agronomists, FPO staff and educated landowners" (council-q1:25)
  • direct: "phones are shared and read aloud by a son, so device ID isn't the farmer"; "text prompts fail low-literacy users, so use voice or icons in Hindi/Pahari" (council-q4:45)
  • direct: "KVKs and FPOs as channels for spreading the word only." (STRATEGY.md:23). "scope must be one district and one crop" (council-q1:42)
  • external: The Nauni and Palampur agromet units reach about 5,000 farmers through 50 WhatsApp groups spread by Krishi Sakhis (Tribune, HP). Digital Green's Farmer.Chat started with extension workers.
Rationale
This answers the councils' open question of how a small OSS team reaches farmers at all. It uses a trusted face-to-face network that already exists and stays inside the "channels only" boundary. The voice script is the concrete content spec behind STRATEGY's vague "Hindi/Pahari voice and icons", and the Android app's notifications can reuse it later.
Downsides
It depends on one Sakhi network agreeing to forward. That is a partnership the team hasn't secured. The audio needs a real Pahari voice, and TTS quality there is unproven. Cards forwarded after their expiry spread stale advice, so the expiry must be shown on the card itself. And because of peer spread, one bad call damages the channel ("dies with bad results", council-q4:33).

I5. "Challenge this call": agronomist review in public

I5Axis 1 · Arrival & channelsConfidence 60%Complexity Low
Description
Give the visitors who actually show up (agronomists, KVK scientists, NGO staff) a job. Every call page links to its rule, inputs and sources, plus a "challenge this call" path that opens a public GitHub issue. Challenges and their resolutions show on the call. A call reviewed against a published review standard can show "reviewed by N agronomists". Reviewer input is kept separate from any farmer baseline.
Basis
  • direct: "advice farmers act on needs KVK or agronomist validation for credibility and liability, which OSS contributors can't provide" (council-q1:42)
  • direct: "Website PoC visitors are agronomists, NGOs and developers, so don't baseline on them." (council-q4:27)
  • direct: The project "should welcome contributions from developers, designers, translators, researchers, agronomists, and farmers." (khetru.md:118)
Rationale
It turns the "wrong audience" problem into the credibility supply the project lacks, without building an officer dashboard. Running it on GitHub keeps it consistent with the open-source positioning, and it costs almost nothing to build.
Downsides
A "reviewed" badge is itself an endorsement claim, so it needs a defined standard or it becomes one more unverifiable claim. GitHub is a barrier for KVK scientists who don't use it. Review volume may be near zero without outreach.

I6. SHC readback: does the card already answer it?

I6Axis 2 · The one-field decision callConfidence 45%Complexity Medium
Description
Build the planned week-one extraction of 20 SHC reports from one district as an open parser and a labelled dataset. On the website, show what a typical card in that district implies for one crop: "how many bags of what, and when". Where the card already prints a crop-wise dose, flag it and use the "Same as your card" verdict (I2) instead of a khetru claim. It is entered by card number or typed values. No on-device OCR in the PoC.
Basis
  • direct: "Farmers hold a 12-parameter lab report but buy fertilizer on the dealer's or a neighbour's word, because the card doesn't turn into 'how many bags of what, for this crop, plot and season.'" (council-q1:22)
  • direct: "pull 20 SHC reports from one district and interview 15 card holders and 2 dealers." (council-q1:47). "Risk: khetru may just repeat the SHC." (council-q4:27)
  • external: IDinsight's SHC diagnostic: farmers don't understand or trust the card, it carries no timing guidance, and the study recommends a redesigned card.
Rationale
The fertilizer decision is where the councils say khetru must beat the dealer or is "done". This is the cheapest falsification test: either khetru adds something beyond the card, or the honest answer is "same as your card". The parser and dataset are public goods that fit the open-platform track.
Downsides
Turning lab values into a dose is a claim the Evidence track can't verify from public observations, which strains the STRATEGY boundary. Cards are old and grid-sampled. Typing values in is work for the farmer. The result may well be that khetru adds nothing here. That is a valuable finding but not a feature.
